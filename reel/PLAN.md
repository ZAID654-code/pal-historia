# Kevin: 30 Days To The Wedding — series plan

## Rules for every episode

- Length 60–100 s. HUD always on: `DAYS LEFT` counts down, `GLOW-UP %` goes up.
- Viewers vote at the end with ONE word. Next day opens with the winner.
- Losing option is not thrown away. It comes back later as a worse problem.
- Emily texts every 3–4 days. Kevin's replies get slightly less bad each time.
- Glow-up never goes up smoothly. Every win has a small loss the same day.

## Story arc used (from the reference video)

Old arc: start at 0, slow rise, one climax. People scroll before it gets good.

Modern arc, used in every episode:

| Time | Level | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | 70/100 | Hook. Dropped into the middle of something already happening. No intro, no recap. |
| 5–15 s | 90/100 | First peak. Contrast, shock stat or contrarian line. |
| 15–40 s | release | Tension drops. Jokes, small steps. |
| 40–50 s | peak 2 | New problem bigger than the first. |
| 50–75 s | release | Kevin deals with it badly. |
| 75–85 s | peak 3 | Twist or Emily text. Re-hook. |
| 85–end | vote | Two options, one word each. |

Never open with "Day X of 30". The day number sits in the HUD only.

## Vote system (replaces the fixed day-by-day table)

Videos are NOT made ahead. Each day: read the vote, build that one episode.
So the plan is per FIX, not per day. Every outcome is covered.

**How the menu works**

1. Each episode ends with two words: A or B.
2. Winner = next episode.
3. Loser stays on the menu and faces the next challenger from the queue below.
4. A fix that loses twice goes to the "comes back worse" pile and returns on a disaster day.
5. Tie, or comments split evenly: DOUBLE FIX day. Both happen in one episode, both go wrong, +2% only.
6. Comments ask for something not on the menu (like "shower" on day 1): it jumps to the front of the queue.
7. Every fix stays for good and is visible in every later episode.

## Episode template v2 (changed after Day 2 retention data)

Day 2 graph: 100% to ~45% in the first 10 s, then flat at ~35% until 0:45, ~10% at the end.
Reading: the story holds people; the opening loses them. The drop covers the hook AND
the 7-second dark results card. So:

| Time | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3 s | Static shot, narrator starts | Motion on frame 1 (something already moving), first caption on screen at 0.0 s |
| 3-5 s | Hook line continues | Peak line |
| 5-12 s | Full-screen results card, 7 s | Results as a 2-second overlay on the scene, no cut to a dark card |
| middle | Peak / release | Keep. This part worked. |
| ~60% in | nothing | Small "A or B?" badge appears and stays, so people who leave early still see the vote |
| end | Vote, last 12 s | Vote, last 6 s |
| Length | 55-72 s | 35-45 s |
