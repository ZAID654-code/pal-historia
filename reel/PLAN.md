# Kevin vs A Wedding — full production plan

30 days. One ugly start. Viewers vote every fix. Day 30 is the wedding.
Videos are built one day at a time, after the vote is in. This file holds everything
needed to build any day, whichever way the vote goes.

Contents: 1 Rules · 2 Episode template · 3 Day-by-day schedule · 4 Fix cards ·
5 Special episodes · 6 Emily thread · 7 Running jokes · 8 Posting · 9 Daily routine

---

## 1. Rules

1. Every episode ends with two words: A or B. Winner is the next episode.
2. Loser stays on the menu and faces the next challenger (see schedule).
3. A fix that loses 3 times leaves the menu and returns as the Day 28 disaster.
4. Tie: DOUBLE FIX day. Both happen, both go wrong, normal % gain.
5. Off-menu word asked for by several commenters: it replaces the next challenger.
6. After a special-vote day, the fix left on the menu happens automatically next day.
7. Deadline fixes: HAIRCUT and SUIT must be done by Day 26. If still unpicked, Kevin panics and does it.
8. Every fix stays visible for good (white teeth, no flies, clean sheets...).
9. Glow-up % belongs to the DAY, not the fix (table in section 3). So every branch lands on 100 at Day 30.
10. Never invent vote numbers on screen. Show "X WON" only.

## 2. Episode template (v3, from Day 2 and Day 3 retention data)

Length 40-50 s. A stranger must understand it with zero context.

| Time | Beat | What is on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 s | STAMP | Big stamp slams in: the one fact that sells the premise ("LAST SHOWER: 2019"). Voice says it. Scene already moving. |
| ~3 s | YOUR VOTE | 2-second overlay "X WON" over the scene. Voice: "You voted to fix that." |
| ~5 s | PEAK 1 | The thing going wrong right now. |
| 7-20 s | GAGS | 4 gags, one every 3-4 s, each bigger than the last. Third one involves another person. |
| ~45% | VOTE BADGE | Pill "NEXT: A OR B?" appears under the HUD and stays. |
| 20-27 s | REWARD | The satisfying part. A meter drains or fills. Glow-up ticks +1% at a time with rising coin sounds, on a three-beat line ("layer by layer by layer"). Kevin visibly changes. |
| 27-32 s | REVEAL | Steam or flash, before/after, ACHIEVEMENT banner "FIX N OF 30 UNLOCKED", confetti. |
| 32-38 s | TWIST | Emily text (on Emily days) or the cliffhanger that sets up the vote. |
| last 5 s | VOTE | Split screen, two words, "you decide day N". |

Always on screen: DAYS LEFT, GLOW-UP %, "KEVIN VS A WEDDING" tag.
Optional, good for comments: 2-second "TOP COMMENT" card showing a real comment from yesterday.

**Rules**
- The two vote words in the caption must be the exact two words the video ends on.
- Same 5 hashtags every day.
- Cover frame: the STAMP frame (second 0).
- Post at the same time daily, when followers are online (Insights → Audience).
- Reply to every comment in the first hour.
- Do not pin "TEAM A / TEAM B" comments. Count the words people write.

**Numbers to watch (send these daily)**
- Retention at 10 s. Day 2 was 45%. Target 60%+.
- Retention at the end. Day 2 was 10%. Target 25%+.
- Comment rate. Day 2 was 0.6%. Target 2%+.
