# Kevin: 30 Days To The Wedding — series plan

## Rules for every episode

- Length 60–100 s. HUD always on: `DAYS LEFT` counts down, `GLOW-UP %` goes up.
- Viewers vote at the end with ONE word. Next day opens with the winner.
- Losing option is not thrown away. It comes back later as a worse problem.
- Emily texts every 3–4 days. Kevin's replies get slightly less bad each time.
- Glow-up never goes up smoothly. Every win has a small loss the same day.

## Story arc used (from the reference video)

Old arc: start at 0, slow rise, one climax. People scroll before it gets good.

Modern arc, used in every episode:

| Time | Level | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | 70/100 | Hook. Dropped into the middle of something already happening. No intro, no recap. |
| 5–15 s | 90/100 | First peak. Contrast, shock stat or contrarian line. |
| 15–40 s | release | Tension drops. Jokes, small steps. |
| 40–50 s | peak 2 | New problem bigger than the first. |
| 50–75 s | release | Kevin deals with it badly. |
| 75–85 s | peak 3 | Twist or Emily text. Re-hook. |
| 85–end | vote | Two options, one word each. |

Never open with "Day X of 30". The day number sits in the HUD only.

## Day 2 — hook scheme and script

Written for GYM winning. Haircut variant below.

| Time | Arc | Screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | **70** hook, mid-story | Kevin flat on gym floor under a barbell with no plates on it. Staff standing over him. | "Kevin has been under this bar for eleven minutes. There is no weight on it." |
| 4–12 s | **90** peak 1 | Vote card flashes: GYM 71%. Cut back to Kevin. HUD: 29 / 0%. | "You did this. Seventy-one percent of you voted gym. The other twenty-nine wanted a haircut. They were right." |
| 12–35 s | release | Rewind: front desk, free trial form, he writes "goblin" under occupation. Treadmill set to 1. He still falls off. | "Thirty minutes earlier. Free trial. One treadmill. Speed one. Kevin lasts ninety seconds, which is a personal best." |
| 35–45 s | **peak 2** | Huge gym guy walks over. Kevin braces. Guy hands him a towel. | "Then the biggest man in the building walks over. Kevin prepares to die. The man hands him a towel. And says: 'First day is the hardest, king.'" |
| 45–68 s | release | Kevin does one push-up. Gym claps. Sweat puddle. The puddle has a smell line. Glow-up ticks 0 → 3%. | "One push-up. An actual one. Three percent. The gym smells worse than when he arrived. That is not the gym's fault." |
| 68–80 s | **peak 3** re-hook | Phone buzzes. Emily: "wait are you at the GYM?? my cousin just saw you 😭" | "And then Emily texts. Her cousin works here. Her cousin saw everything." |
| 80–95 s | vote | Split screen: SHOWER / LAUNDRY. | "He cannot fix what she saw. But he can fix the smell. Does he shower? Or wash the clothes he has worn since March? One word. Day three starts with your choice." |

- Peak 2: the cape comes off, half the head is done, power cut.
- Peak 3: Emily: "omg is that you in the window of Sharp Cuts".
- Same vote: SHOWER / LAUNDRY.
